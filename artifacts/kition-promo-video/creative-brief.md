# Kition Promo Video Creative Brief

## Product

Kition is an AI workspace that brings Markdown documents, structured
tables, an action-capable agent, and visual workflows into one desktop app.

## Audience

Knowledge workers, creators, small teams, and developers who want AI to work
with their real files and structured data without moving their workspace into a
cloud-only proprietary format.

## One-Line Promise

Your documents, data, and AI workflows can live and work together on your own
machine.

## Safe Claims

- Plain Markdown documents remain files on disk.
- Structured tables live alongside documents in the same workspace.
- The agent can work with documents, tables, tools, and workflows.
- Users can configure their preferred model provider.
- Kition is designed as an integrated desktop workspace.

## Primary Visual Carrier

Real Kition UI macro shots in the product's dark-default visual system: a
near-black canvas, layered dark panels, restrained borders, and project-purple
accents.

## Reference Direction

UI-macro launch film with concise kinetic typography, fast evidence shots,
camera push-ins, lateral wipes, and a clean brand lockup. The product remains
the hero rather than decorative generated imagery.

## Shot Rhythm

1. Product capability reveal.
2. First-run onboarding files and Welcome document.
3. Structured table proof.
4. Agent proof.
5. Workflow automation proof.
6. Product-value brand close.

## Voice And Subtitle Approach

Music-first cut with no narration. This avoids shipping low-quality system TTS.
Short on-screen copy carries the story, so no subtitle track is required.

## Quality Risks

- The table and workflow source captures use audit data rather than a polished
  customer workspace; their neutral surfaces are mapped to Kition's dark
  palette while preserving brand and semantic colors.
- The Welcome scene is a real dark-theme capture of the shipped onboarding files.
- The Agent scene is derived from a real product recording and stored as a
  repository-local source asset.
- The soundtrack is third-party stock music under the Mixkit Stock Music Free
  License; its source and license are recorded in `music-source.md`.
- Motion is produced with FFmpeg rather than a reusable 3D curved-screen rig.
